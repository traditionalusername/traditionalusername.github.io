Why I Built This Project

I built this project to create a quiet-respectful space to remember our current generation of Princeton students. Throughout the Chapel, you will find the names of many tigers from previous generations, memorializing them and their contribution. During my enrollment as a student, these tigers stood out above the rest, conveying the purpose and privilege of our time spent at this University.
